The Ipanema Method

Saúde Capilar
Acima de Tudo

Hair health comes first. Beauty follows with more confidence.

The Ipanema Method is our philosophy of care. Before recommending a service, we consider the condition of your hair, scalp, lifestyle, comfort, and desired result. This health-first approach helps us decide when extensions are appropriate, when the scalp needs attention first, and when a private appointment will create a better experience.

Our goal is not to sell the most service. Our goal is to guide you toward the right service.

Hair & Scalp · Free Analysis

Start with clarity before choosing your next hair service.

If you are experiencing hair loss, scalp sensitivity, visible scalp concerns, postpartum shedding, stress-related changes, or uncertainty about extensions, the Hair & Scalp Free Analysis is the most thoughtful place to begin.

This appointment helps you understand what may be happening, what your hair can safely receive, and which next step supports your goals — without compromising your hair health.

We do not make medical promises or diagnose medical conditions. We provide a careful beauty and scalp wellness assessment and, when appropriate, recommend that clients seek medical guidance.

Private Beauty Experience

For moments when beauty requires more privacy.

Some beauty experiences require more than technique. They require discretion, sensitivity, and a space where you do not have to explain yourself twice.

Private appointments are available for clients who prefer or require a more discreet experience — including religious or cultural privacy needs, medical hair loss, wig or scarf removal, gender-affirming beauty support, visible scalp concerns, or any personal reason that makes privacy important.

At Ipanema Beauty Bar, privacy is offered with respect. No judgment. No pressure. Just attentive care.

Reserve a Private Consultation

You may share your privacy needs in the booking form, or simply request a private appointment and we will guide you with discretion.
